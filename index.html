Latency

Each new recording is shifted back by this much. Save one value per combination of headphones and microphone. Existing recordings keep their own value, which you change in the track's settings.

Measure

Exact. Ten clicks are played and the app listens for them, so no human timing is involved. Wear your headphones, turn the volume up and hold the phone's microphone, on the bottom edge, right against one earbud. With a speaker, just stay quiet.

By clapping. Keep the headphones in and clap on every click. People tend to clap slightly ahead of a click, so this usually reads tens of milliseconds shorter than the exact value.

Keys and pedal

Space starts and stops. R, Enter or → records. ← undoes the latest recording. C captures the last lap. K toggles the click. 1 to 9 mute a track. A Bluetooth pedal that sends arrow keys therefore works as a record pedal.
