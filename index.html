Varv is a looper: what you record repeats in time with the click, layer by layer. Tap Record: one bar of count-in, then it records lap after lap, each into the next empty track, until you tap Record again. Hold Record to start or stop without recording.

Bluetooth headphones lag, so Varv moves each recording back to land on the beat. Measure the delay once: swipe left, Measure exactly.

Tap a track to mute it. Hold it to record one lap into it, from its next lap; hold again to cancel. Tap its name to solo it.

Swipe right to edit a track's sound and timing, left for settings. Tap the logo to come back.

Undo removes the latest recording, and the bar that appears can bring it back. Capture last keeps the lap you just played, even without pressing Record.

Guide

Keys and pedal

Space, R, Enter or → work like the Record button: tap to record, hold to start or stop. ← or Backspace undo. C capture last. K click. 1–9 mute a track, Shift+1–9 solo it. E edit, S settings, Esc back to play. P record the performance. A Bluetooth pedal that sends any of the record keys works as a record pedal.
