Tap Record. The click counts in one bar, then one lap is recorded. Each new lap becomes its own layer, shifted back by the latency so it lands on the beat.

Latency

Each new layer is shifted back by this much. Save one value per combination of headphones and microphone. Existing layers keep their own value, which you change on the layer.

Measure

Exact. Ten clicks are played and the app listens for them, so no human timing is involved. Wear your headphones, turn the volume up and hold the phone's microphone, on the bottom edge, right against one earbud. With a speaker, just stay quiet.

By clapping. Keep the headphones in and clap on every click. People tend to clap slightly ahead of a click, so this usually reads tens of milliseconds shorter than the exact value.

Mix send feeds the whole mix into the delay or reverb. Each layer also has its own EQ, filter, delay and reverb: tap a layer's strip to open them. Double-tap a slider to reset it. The compressor delays click and layers by the same few milliseconds, so timing is unaffected.

Keys and pedal

Space starts and stops. R, Enter or → records. ← undoes the latest layer. C captures the last lap. K toggles the click. 1 to 9 mute a layer. A Bluetooth pedal that sends arrow keys therefore works as a record pedal.
